Customers can return an item within 30 days of purchase, provided they have a
receipt and the item is unused.[^owner-statement-2026-09-14]

## Conditions for a return

- The return is made within 30 days of purchase.
- A receipt is provided.
- The item is unused.

## Refund timing

Once a return is accepted, the refund lands within 5 working
days.[^owner-statement-2026-09-14]

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are exempt from the 30-day return window.[^owner-statement-2026-09-14]

> Open question: whether a receipt is still required, and whether the item

CreativeDev is an "AI-Native Software Studio" led by founder Sharmeen Fatima.
The studio builds intelligent web platforms that automate workflows and
engage customers, distinguishing itself from static templates through
autonomous AI integration.[^creativedev-store-2026-09-14]

## Services

- AI-Powered Websites & Web Apps — Next.js applications with embedded AI
  capabilities for personalization and smart search.
- AI Agents & Intelligent Chatbots — autonomous agents with tool calling and
  multi-step reasoning.
- Automation & Digital AI Employees — 24/7 agents across email, WhatsApp,
  CRM/ERP, and social channels.
- Full-Stack Web Development — Next.js frontends paired with Python/FastAPI
  backends.
- UI/UX & High-Performance Frontend — responsive design with
  micro-interactions.
- Cybersecurity-Aware Development — OWASP-informed engineering
  practices.[^creativedev-store-2026-09-14]

## Technical stack

- AI & Agents: OpenAI Agents SDK, Model Context Protocol (MCP), RAG
  pipelines, multi-agent systems.
- Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.
- Backend: Python, FastAPI, relational databases.
- Security: OWASP guidelines, CSP headers, Zod validation.[^creativedev-store-2026-09-14]

## Working philosophy

A 5-phase process: Discover & Map, Design & Spec, Build & Integrate,
Hardening & Test, Launch & Support.[^creativedev-store-2026-09-14]
